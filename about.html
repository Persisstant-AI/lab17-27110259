A little more about us

Student life is a lot to carry on your own.

Persisstant is being built to offer thoughtful support through the busy weeks, big plans, and small moments that make up student life. A gentle nudge can help you feel a little more ready for what’s next.

Support, without taking over

We believe useful help should fit around your life, not add another thing to manage. Persisstant is designed to help you remember what matters and break a full week into kinder, more manageable steps.

Made for the whole picture

Student life is more than classes. It’s preparing for exams, keeping up with people you love, and finding a moment to look ahead. We’re bringing those everyday pieces together with care.
